MAJOR CATALYST — AD Ports Group Mandatory Tender Offer (December 2025)

Abu Dhabi Ports Group (ADX: ADPORTS) announced a cash Mandatory Tender Offer (MTO) to acquire a controlling stake in ALCN after purchasing a 19.3% block from Saudi PIF-owned SEIC for EGP 13.2 billion in November 2025. To gain majority control, AD Ports needs an additional ~32% stake from the open market. This structural M&A event is the key near-term re-rating catalyst and signals strategic premium for ALCN. Egyptian government shareholders have committed to maintain their existing stakes.

Investment Recommendation

Synthesizing multi-method valuation, financial fundamentals, AD Ports MTO catalyst, and Egypt macro context

▲ SHORT-TERM (3–12 months): ACCUMULATE / BUY
Near-Term Thesis: AD Ports MTO Catalyst + Valuation Gap

The AD Ports Group MTO is the dominant near-term catalyst. AD Ports paid EGP 13.2B for 19.3% of ALCN (Nov 2025) — an implied whole-company valuation of ~EGP 68B. For the MTO to succeed and acquire the ~32% needed for majority control, AD Ports must offer a premium to market, historically 15–25% above current price, implying a range of 28–30+ EGP per share.

Our blended fair value of 27.0 EGP implies +10.9% upside from current 24.35 EGP. The DDM model at 25.6 EGP (+5%) confirms the stock is near or below intrinsic value. The 8%+ dividend yield provides strong income support while waiting for the M&A event to materialize.

BUY / ACCUMULATE — especially for dividend-income and event-driven investors. Entry zone: 22.5–24.5 EGP · Target: 27–30 EGP · Stop-loss: 21.5 EGP

◆ LONG-TERM (2–5 years): HOLD WITH UPSIDE
Structural Strengths vs. EGP Currency & Growth Risks

Long-term, ALCN benefits from Egypt's geography as a critical Suez Canal hub. Revenue grew 194% (FY22→FY25) primarily driven by USD-denominated TEU pricing gains as EGP devalued. This provides a natural USD-inflation hedge for Egyptian investors.

Risks: H1 FY25/26 shows -2% net profit and -4% revenue decline (normalization). The ~99% payout ratio leaves zero retained capital for expansion without external funding. EGP depreciation risk materially compresses USD-equivalent returns for foreign investors. Only 2 analysts cover the stock (limited institutional discovery).

HOLD / ACCUMULATE ON WEAKNESS — Long-term fair value range: 26–32 EGP over 3 years, driven by AD Ports integration efficiencies, Suez trade volume recovery, and potential tariff re-pricing in USD terms.
